\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented \toolname, a SAT-based algorithm for mining \GRone
specifications from lasso traces.
By fixing the temporal skeleton of the \GRone template and encoding
only propositional content, \toolname eliminates the need to search
over temporal operators and produces formulas that are directly
suitable for reactive synthesis.
An environment--system variable partition ensures that mined
specifications respect the assume--guarantee boundary, and
incremental SAT solving enables efficient enumeration over template
configurations.

On 60 boolean Spectra benchmarks with 16--218 variables, \toolname
solves 58/60 within a 10-minute timeout, scaling to
218~variables---well beyond the reach of ATLAS, which solves
at most 37/60 (unconstrained) or 25/60 (\GRone-constrained)
and cannot handle any benchmark beyond 76~variables.

Future work includes extending the trace generator and miner to
larger variable counts, incorporating a CEGIS loop that
iteratively refines the mined specification against a ground-truth
oracle, and supporting richer Spectra specifications through
automatic booleanisation of enumerated types and integer variables.
