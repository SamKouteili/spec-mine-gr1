\begin{abstract}
We present \toolname, a SAT-based tool for mining \GRone specifications
from lasso-shaped traces. \GRone formulas---of the form
$(\varphi_I^e \land \always \varphi_S^e \land \bigwedge_i \always\eventually J_i) \rightarrow
(\varphi_I^s \land \always \varphi_S^s \land \bigwedge_j \always\eventually G_j)$---are
the fragment of LTL for which reactive synthesis is polynomial, yet no
prior tool mines them directly from traces.  Existing LTL miners search
over both temporal and propositional operators, treating GR(1) as just
another LTL formula.  We exploit the fixed temporal skeleton of \GRone:
the temporal structure is given by the template, so only
propositional sub-formulas need to be synthesised.  This eliminates the
temporal operator semantics---the dominant cost in general LTL
mining---and decomposes a single large SAT instance into several
small, independent ones.
On a benchmark suite of 20~\GRone specifications, \toolname solves
all~20 while the general LTL miner times out on~4, with speedups
ranging from $3.5\times$ to over $15{,}000\times$.
Moreover, the \GRone template guarantees that every mined formula is
directly usable for reactive synthesis, whereas the baseline frequently
produces formulas outside any known realisability fragment.
\end{abstract}
